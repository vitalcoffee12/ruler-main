\# The Pearl Kingdom of Thalassara

\## Overview

Far beneath the surface of the world lies Thalassara, an ancient kingdom hidden within the endless depths of the Sapphire Expanse.

To surface dwellers, the ocean is a vast wilderness.

To the people of Thalassara, it is a living empire stretching across countless trenches, reefs, ruins, and forgotten cities swallowed by time.

The kingdom is inhabited by the Merathi, a race of half-human, half-beast seafolk whose appearances reflect the creatures of the ocean. Some possess the tails of fish, others the limbs of crustaceans, the fins of sharks, the tentacles of cephalopods, or the graceful forms of rays and sea dragons.

The Merathi believe the sea remembers everything.

Every shipwreck, every death, every kingdom lost to the waves becomes part of an eternal memory carried by the currents.

Throughout the ocean floor lie the remains of civilizations far older than humanity. Entire palaces, temples, and cities rest in darkness, preserved beneath layers of coral and stone. Many of these ruins are considered sacred. Others are feared. Some are forbidden to approach entirely.

\---



\## Region in Thalassara

\### The Capital of Nacreon

The heart of Thalassara is the city of Nacreon.

Built within the skeletal remains of a colossal sea creature long extinct, the city glows with pale bioluminescent light.

Towering spires of pearl and coral rise from the creature's ribs.

Ancient streets weave through chambers large enough to contain entire villages.

The city is never silent.

Whale songs echo through distant halls.

Schools of silver fish drift through open plazas.

Priests chant beneath glowing domes formed from translucent shells.

At the city's center stands the Palace of Tides, home of the royal family.

Its walls are covered with mosaics depicting the rise and fall of forgotten ages.

Many sections of the palace are older than recorded history.

No one remembers who first built them.



\### The Coral Labyrinth

A sprawling maze of coral mountains stretching for hundreds of miles.

Brightly colored reefs conceal hidden settlements, sacred shrines, and dangerous predators.

Travelers can easily become lost among constantly shifting passages.

Entire clans make their homes here.

Many never leave.



\### The Gravewater Basin

A vast field of ancient shipwrecks.

Thousands of vessels from countless eras rest upon the seafloor.

Some ships are so old that their origins are unknown.

Treasure hunters often venture into the basin seeking relics.

Many vanish.

Locals claim the dead sailors still wander among the wrecks.



\### The Obsidian Trench

A seemingly bottomless abyss descending into darkness.

Light cannot penetrate its depths.

Even the bravest explorers avoid its lower regions.

Strange sounds occasionally rise from below.

Some resemble voices.

Others resemble songs.

None sound entirely human.



\### The Kingdom of Glass Reefs

A region filled with crystal formations growing naturally from the seabed.

Entire forests of translucent spires stretch into the darkness.

The reefs produce haunting melodies whenever ocean currents pass through them.

Many consider the area sacred.

Others believe the sounds are warnings.



\### The Drowned Empire of Valemyr

The ruins of a civilization that existed before Thalassara.

Its marble towers remain remarkably intact despite the passing millennia.

Massive statues watch silently over empty avenues.

No records explain how the empire fell.

Many buildings remain sealed.

No one knows what lies within.



\## The Merathi Peoples

\### The Selkari

Seal-like seafolk known for their adaptability and curiosity.

They frequently interact with surface cultures and often serve as traders and diplomats.

They are widely regarded as storytellers and explorers.



\### The Lamnari

Shark-born warriors.

Powerful, disciplined, and feared throughout the ocean.

Many serve in the royal military.

Despite their reputation, they value honor above violence.



\### The Cephari

Octopus and squid-like Merathi.

Masters of scholarship, art, and ancient knowledge.

Many possess unusual abilities involving illusion and memory.

They maintain the largest libraries in the kingdom.



\### The Drakari

Rare descendants of ancient sea dragons.

Larger and longer-lived than most Merathi.

They often occupy positions of authority.

Legends claim their bloodline predates the kingdom itself.



\### The Nymari

Ray-like seafolk who inhabit the deepest regions.

Quiet and mysterious.

Many surface dwellers mistake them for spirits.

They rarely speak of what exists in the lower depths.



\## Important Figures

\### Queen Nerissa VII

The current ruler of Thalassara.

Wise, patient, and deeply respected.

She has ruled for nearly eighty years.

Many believe she knows secrets about the kingdom's origins that she has never revealed.



\### Prince Kaelor

Heir to the throne.

Young, adventurous, and fascinated by surface civilizations.

His frequent expeditions beyond the kingdom often place him at odds with traditionalists.



\### Archivist Veyra

Keeper of the Abyssal Archives.

She oversees countless relics recovered from ancient ruins.

Her knowledge of lost civilizations is unmatched.

Many suspect she knows far more than she shares.



\### Admiral Rhask

Commander of the Tideguard.

A renowned Lamnari warrior.

Veteran of numerous conflicts against deep-sea predators and hostile clans.

His loyalty to the crown is unquestioned.



\## Creatures of the Deep

\### Moonwhales

Gigantic creatures whose glowing bodies illuminate entire regions during migration seasons.

Their songs can be heard across hundreds of miles.

Many consider them sacred.



\### Crown Leviathans

Ancient predators larger than cities.

Fortunately, sightings are extremely rare.

Legends describe them as older than the oceans themselves.



\### Mirror Eels

Long silver creatures capable of mimicking voices they hear.

They often lure travelers away from safe routes.



\### Reef Giants

Massive beings resembling living coral formations.

Most remain motionless for decades.

No one is entirely certain whether they are animals, spirits, or something else.



\## Legends and Rumors

\- An entire city exists beneath the Obsidian Trench.

\- The sea floor hides gates leading to other worlds.

\- The royal family descends from an ancient ocean deity.

\- Some ruins predate the moon.

\- A sleeping leviathan rests beneath Nacreon itself.

\- Certain pearls contain memories from previous ages.

\- The Drowned Empire of Valemyr did not fall—it simply disappeared.



\## The Cult of the Last Current

A secretive religious movement found throughout the kingdom.

Its followers believe the ocean is slowly dying.

According to their teachings, a final current will one day sweep across the world and carry all living things into a new age.

Many dismiss them as mystics.

Others fear they may know something the rest of the kingdom has forgotten.



\## Atmosphere

Thalassara is a realm of wonder, silence, and ancient mystery.

Sunlight rarely reaches its deepest regions.

Ruins from countless lost civilizations emerge from darkness like forgotten dreams.

History is everywhere.

Entire generations live among monuments built by peoples whose names have vanished.

The ocean conceals more than it reveals.

Every trench descends into uncertainty.

Every ruin hides unanswered questions.

And somewhere in the deepest parts of the sea, beyond maps and memory, something ancient still waits beneath the ruins.
