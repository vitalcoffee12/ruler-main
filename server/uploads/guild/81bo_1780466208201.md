\# The Republic of Korea After the Break



\---



\# Overview



Fifteen years ago, the world changed.



Without warning, glowing fissures appeared across the globe.



At first, they were believed to be strange natural phenomena. Scientists studied them. Governments quarantined them. News stations broadcast them endlessly.



Then the first Gate opened.



Creatures unlike any known species emerged from within.



Entire districts disappeared overnight.



Military forces proved ineffective against many of the monsters, and humanity faced the possibility of extinction.



Everything changed when certain individuals awakened supernatural abilities.



These people became known as Awakened.



The event would later be remembered simply as:



\*\*The Break.\*\*



\---



\# Modern Korea



The Republic of Korea survived the chaos better than most nations.



Strong infrastructure, rapid government response, and an unusually high number of Awakened allowed the country to recover.



Today, Korea stands as one of the world's leading powers in the post-Break era.



The skyline of Seoul remains recognizable.



Subways still run.



Universities still operate.



Office workers still complain about overtime.



Yet behind the appearance of normalcy lies a society permanently transformed.



Monster attacks occasionally occur.



Gates can appear without warning.



National news broadcasts regularly discuss dungeon incidents alongside weather forecasts and stock markets.



For most citizens, supernatural disasters have become part of everyday life.



\---



\# Seoul



The largest city in Korea and the center of Awakened activity.



Towering guild headquarters dominate the skyline alongside corporate offices.



Digital billboards display real-time Gate alerts.



Luxury apartments owned by elite hunters overlook districts where low-ranked Awakened struggle to survive.



The city never truly sleeps.



Monster outbreaks, dungeon expeditions, black-market auctions, and guild recruitment operations continue around the clock.



Many young people dream of awakening powerful abilities and joining prestigious guilds.



Few succeed.



\---



\# Busan



A coastal city that experienced some of the most devastating incidents during the early years of the Break.



Several districts were destroyed by sea-born monsters emerging from underwater Gates.



Although rebuilt, the city remains heavily fortified.



Massive barriers stand along portions of the coastline.



Stories persist of strange creatures lurking beneath the waves beyond patrol zones.



\---



\# Incheon



One of the nation's most important transportation hubs.



International Gates occasionally connect to regions beyond Earth, making Incheon a center of research and trade.



Foreign hunters, scholars, diplomats, and merchants frequently pass through the city.



Many of the country's largest artifact exchanges occur here.



\---



\# Jeju Island



Officially a tourist destination.



Unofficially one of the most dangerous regions in the nation.



Numerous high-level Gates have appeared on the island over the years.



Entire sections remain restricted to civilians.



Many hunters regard Jeju as cursed.



Others see it as a land of opportunity.



\---



\# The Gates



No one fully understands where Gates originate.



Some appear in cities.



Others emerge in mountains, oceans, deserts, or abandoned buildings.



Every Gate leads somewhere different.



Some contain ruined civilizations.



Some contain endless wilderness.



Some appear completely devoid of life.



A few contain structures that seem deliberately constructed.



The most unsettling Gates are those that contain evidence of human habitation despite having no known connection to Earth.



\---



\# Dungeons



The spaces beyond Gates are collectively called Dungeons.



Many resemble fantasy worlds.



Others appear disturbingly modern.



Hunters have reported finding abandoned hospitals, subway systems, schools, office buildings, and entire cities within certain Dungeons.



Some locations show signs of sudden evacuation.



Others appear frozen in time.



No explanation has ever been confirmed.



\---



\# The Hunter Association



The official government organization responsible for regulating Awakened activities.



The Association licenses hunters, monitors Gates, investigates dungeon incidents, and coordinates national emergency responses.



Most citizens trust the organization.



Most hunters do not.



Rumors of corruption, hidden research projects, and political interference circulate constantly.



\---



\# Major Guilds



\---



\## Baekho Guild



One of Korea's oldest and most prestigious guilds.



Known for discipline, military-style operations, and exceptional combat capability.



Many national heroes emerged from its ranks.



\---



\## Mirae Guild



A wealthy organization specializing in dungeon exploration and artifact acquisition.



Its influence extends into politics, finance, and media.



Many critics argue the guild behaves more like a corporation than a public institution.



\---



\## Cheongryong Guild



A powerful guild composed primarily of elite combat specialists.



Their members are celebrities throughout the country.



Young hunters often idolize them.



Veteran hunters tend to view them more cautiously.



\---



\## Haneul Guild



A rapidly growing organization focused on research, logistics, and support operations.



Many scholars and non-combat Awakened work under its banner.



\---



\# Important Figures



\## Chairman Han Seong-Min



Current head of the Hunter Association.



Calm, intelligent, and politically skilled.



He publicly advocates transparency.



Privately, many believe he knows far more about the origins of the Gates than he admits.



\---



\## Kang Ji-Hoon



Korea's highest-ranked active hunter.



Widely regarded as a living legend.



He rarely appears in public.



Most information regarding his activities remains classified.



\---



\## Yoo Seo-Ah



A rising hunter whose rapid growth has attracted national attention.



Many consider her the next great hero of Korea.



Others fear the pace of her development is unnatural.



\---



\## Professor Kim Do-Won



One of the foremost researchers studying Gates.



His theories regarding the nature of Dungeons are considered controversial.



Several of his former colleagues have disappeared under mysterious circumstances.



\---



\# The Abyss Zones



Certain locations have experienced repeated Gate activity for years.



These regions gradually change.



Plants mutate.



Animals behave strangely.



Reality itself appears unstable.



Locals refer to such areas as Abyss Zones.



The government officially denies many reported phenomena.



Witnesses continue reporting them regardless.



\---



\# The Black Market



A hidden network operating throughout the country.



Illegal artifacts, forbidden information, monster materials, and stolen dungeon relics circulate through underground channels.



Powerful hunters, politicians, corporations, and criminal organizations all maintain connections to the market.



Official crackdowns occur regularly.



None have succeeded in eliminating it.



\---



\# Unanswered Mysteries



\- Why did the Break begin?

\- Who created the Dungeons?

\- Why do some Dungeons resemble Earth?

\- What exists beyond the highest-ranked Gates?

\- Why do certain Awakened hear voices during awakening?

\- Why do some monsters appear to recognize humans?

\- What happened to the first expedition teams that never returned?



\---



\# Rumors



\- A hidden Gate exists somewhere beneath Seoul.

\- Certain S-Rank hunters possess abilities that were never publicly disclosed.

\- Some Dungeons continue functioning even after their Gates are destroyed.

\- Entire civilizations exist beyond the Gates.

\- A dungeon resembling future Earth has been discovered.

\- The first Awakened may have existed before the Break.

\- Something on the other side of the Gates is observing humanity.



\---



\# Atmosphere



Modern Korea is prosperous, technologically advanced, and outwardly stable.



People attend school, work corporate jobs, order food delivery, watch livestreams, and complain about rising housing prices.



Yet beneath ordinary life exists a second world.



A world of hunters, monsters, hidden expeditions, political conspiracies, ancient ruins, and secrets buried inside impossible dimensions.



Most citizens only glimpse fragments of it through news reports and social media.



Those who enter the Gates discover a different truth.



Humanity may not be exploring the Dungeons.



It may be the Dungeons that are slowly exploring humanity.
