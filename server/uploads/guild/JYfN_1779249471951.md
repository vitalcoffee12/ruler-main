\# The World of Elarion



\---



\# Overview



Elarion is a hidden magical realm layered quietly beside the ordinary world.  

Most humans pass their entire lives without ever perceiving it, though traces of its existence linger in forgotten alleyways, abandoned train stations, ruined cathedrals, and forests untouched by maps.



Magic in Elarion is ancient, emotional, and deeply personal.  

It is tied not to strict rules, but to memory, longing, fear, grief, and desire.



The realm itself is old beyond measurement.  

Entire kingdoms have vanished without record, while certain castles, forests, and roads seem to remember every soul that ever walked through them.



At the center of the hidden world stands the oldest magical institution still remaining:



\# The Blackthorn Academy



\---



\# Blackthorn Academy



Blackthorn Academy was built atop cliffs overlooking the cold northern sea.



No one remembers who founded it.



The castle appears differently to each student who arrives for the first time.  

Some remember towering silver gates. Others describe endless stairways descending underground. A few insist the academy first appeared to them in dreams years before their invitation arrived.



The school is vast enough that no student has ever fully explored it.



Entire hallways disappear overnight.  

Locked doors sometimes open only for certain individuals.  

Portraits whisper secrets when no adults are nearby.



At night, distant bells echo from parts of the castle that officially do not exist.



The academy is both sanctuary and prison.



Many students arrive believing they are special.



Most eventually discover the school wanted something from them long before they were born.



\---



\# The Kingdoms Beyond



\## The Ashen Dominion



A cold imperial nation ruled by ancient bloodlines.



Its capital is built around black towers carved from volcanic stone, permanently covered in drifting ash despite the absence of nearby fire.



The ruling families preserve forbidden magical traditions and believe emotion weakens spellcraft.



Children born with unstable magic often disappear before adulthood.



Travelers claim the royal palace has no reflections anywhere inside it.



\---



\## The Vale of Lyth



A quiet region of forests, rivers, and hidden villages.



Magic here is subtle and old-fashioned.  

Locals leave offerings beside ancient trees and speak carefully around rivers at night.



It is said the dead occasionally return during winter, though never violently.  

They simply walk silently through the villages before vanishing at dawn.



Many students at Blackthorn Academy come from the Vale.



\---



\## The City of Vey Moura



The largest magical city in the known world.



Crowded streets twist endlessly beneath glowing lanterns suspended by invisible threads.  

Markets sell bottled dreams, preserved memories, enchanted instruments, and letters that answer back when read aloud.



The city never truly sleeps.



Beneath its beauty lies constant tension between noble houses, criminal syndicates, scholars, and cults devoted to forgotten entities.



People disappear frequently in Vey Moura.



Most are never spoken of again.



\---



\## The Hollow Sea



A dark northern ocean feared even by experienced navigators.



The water is unnaturally still during certain seasons.



Ships crossing the Hollow Sea sometimes report hearing voices beneath the waves speaking in languages no one recognizes.



Entire crews have vanished while their vessels continued drifting untouched.



Blackthorn Academy overlooks this sea.



Some students claim the ocean watches the castle at night.



\---



\# The Whispering Forest



An enormous forest stretching across several kingdoms.



Paths rearrange themselves constantly.



Travelers who enter without permission often wander for weeks before emerging unchanged in body but altered in personality.



The deeper regions contain abandoned shrines, ruined observatories, and creatures older than recorded civilization.



The forest dislikes iron.



Those carrying too much of it rarely find their way home.



\---



\# Important Figures



\## Headmaster Aurelius Vale



The current headmaster of Blackthorn Academy.



Tall, calm, and impossibly difficult to read.



No student has ever seen him visibly angry.



Rumors claim he has led the academy for over two centuries without aging.



He speaks kindly, but many students leave conversations with him feeling deeply unsettled, as though he understood thoughts they never voiced aloud.



\---



\## Seraphine Noct



Professor of Memory Arts.



Known for silver hair, dark gloves, and a habit of finishing students' sentences before they speak.



She rarely punishes misconduct directly.



Instead, students who anger her often discover entire memories missing from their minds for days afterward.



No one knows where she came from.



\---



\## Elias Thorn



A sixth-year student feared throughout the academy.



Brilliant, charismatic, and dangerously ambitious.



He believes the academy hides knowledge powerful enough to reshape death itself.



Several students who followed him into restricted areas were later found unable to remember their own names.



\---



\## Mira Elsin



A quiet girl frequently seen inside the library towers.



She possesses an unusual ability to hear emotions lingering inside objects.



Old books whisper to her constantly.



Despite her fragile appearance, even professors treat her cautiously.



Some believe the castle itself speaks to her while she sleeps.



\---



\## The Faceless Choir



A mysterious group mentioned only in forbidden texts.



Their members supposedly surrendered their identities in exchange for forbidden knowledge.



No confirmed member has ever been discovered.



Yet their symbol — a circle of eyeless faces — occasionally appears carved into academy walls after violent incidents.



\---



\# Legends and Rumors



\- A sealed chamber exists beneath Blackthorn Academy containing something alive.

\- The castle occasionally chooses students who will later disappear permanently.

\- Some mirrors within the academy show events from the future instead of reflections.

\- A forgotten student wanders the halls every winter, though no records of their existence remain.

\- Certain books in the library rewrite themselves overnight.

\- The Hollow Sea once swallowed an entire kingdom during a single storm.

\- The Whispering Forest contains a doorway leading somewhere beyond death.



\---



\# Atmosphere



The world of Elarion is beautiful, ancient, and quietly melancholic.



Magic is wondrous, but never entirely safe.



Most people live ordinary lives while sensing that something vast and unknowable exists just beyond their understanding.



The past is never truly gone here.



Memories linger in buildings.  

Grief shapes landscapes.  

Secrets survive longer than kingdoms.



And somewhere beneath Blackthorn Academy, something ancient is beginning to wake.
